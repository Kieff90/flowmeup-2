# Copy Brief — English
# Flowmeup Landing Page — EN

> Version: 1.0 — 2026-05-23
> Scope: Complete copy brief for Phase 2 builders. Each section has a headline, body direction, and CTA.
> Audience: Business owner or head of sales, SMB, EU English-speaking market, 40-55 years old, sectors: construction, manufacturing, B2B distribution.
> Note: This is an adaptation for EN-speaking EU markets, not a literal translation of the IT brief. Same structure, same specificity, adjusted register.

---

## Section 1: Hero

**Headline:**
Your sales rep logs a lead in 90 seconds. From their phone. While driving.

**Subheadline / body direction:**
For business owners with field sales teams: leads don't go cold because your reps aren't working. They go cold because no one opens a CRM from the car between appointments. Flowmeup fixes that. Nothing to install. Nothing to change.

**CTA:**
Button text: "Let's talk about your process"
Link: anchor to Section 8 (form + calendar)

**Forbidden words check:** no occurrences of chatbot, AREX, persistent context, modular platform, AI-powered CRM, artificial intelligence (generic).

**Notes for the builder:**
- Headline in H1, large, maximum readability on mobile.
- Subheadline should stay under 2 lines on desktop.
- CTA must be in the fold: visible without scrolling on iPhone 12+.
- No animation on headline at load (approved SPEC-02 decision).
- Background: deep navy (#0F2349). Text: white. CTA: amber (#F59E0B).

## Section 2: Problem

**Headline:**
Every day your salespeople lose hours updating spreadsheets and CRMs. On a good day.

**Subheadline / body direction:**
The meeting ends. The rep gets in the car. The CRM is on the laptop back at the office. "I'll update it tonight" — but tonight it doesn't happen. The lead sits there. You find out two weeks later when someone asks about the account and nobody has notes.

This isn't a discipline problem. It's a friction problem: too many steps between when something happens in the field and when it gets recorded anywhere.

**CTA:** no CTA in this section. Narrative flow carries to the next section.

**Forbidden words check:** no occurrences of forbidden terms.

**Notes for the builder:**
- White or very light grey background for contrast after the hero.
- Narrative text, no bullet points.
- 2 paragraphs, readable font size (minimum 17px body).
- The last sentence before the solution should create the tension that pulls to Section 3.

## Section 3: Solution

**Headline:**
AI agents built around your process. They talk to your team. They save where you already save.

**Subheadline / body direction:**
Your rep sends a voice note on WhatsApp. The lead is saved. No app to download, no training session, no new workflow. The agents work where your team already works: WhatsApp, Excel, the spreadsheets you've used for years. Flowmeup sets everything up. Your team is running within a week.

**CTA:** no direct CTA. Section leads organically to the agent cards.

**Forbidden words check:** no occurrences of forbidden terms.

**Notes for the builder:**
- This section introduces the concept before the specific agent cards.
- Tone: reassuring, concrete. Not technical.
- 2-3 small icons (WhatsApp, Excel, calendar) can make "where you already work" visual, but only if they don't slow load time.
- Clean transition into Section 4 (agent cards).

## Section 4: Agent cards

**Section headline:**
The Flowmeup agents

**Section subheadline:**
Live now or coming soon. Each one solves a specific sales process.

**Card 1: Voice Lead**

Badge: LIVE

Card headline: Voice Lead — log a lead in 90 seconds

Before: ~30 minutes (open the tool, find the record, update it, save)
After: 90 seconds

Trigger: Send a voice note. The lead is in your system.

Description: Your rep says "Met Bianchi Construction today, interested in product X, follow up in two weeks." The agent transcribes, extracts the fields, updates the record. Confirmation in 30 seconds.

**Card 2: Lead Scout**

Badge: LIVE

Card headline: Lead Scout — find new prospects in 30 minutes

Before: ~1 day (manual search across Google, LinkedIn, company registries)
After: 30 minutes

Trigger: Describe what you're looking for. Get the list.

Description: "Construction companies in Northern Italy, 20-100 employees, commercial fit-outs." The agent returns a qualified prospect list with company name, sector, location, and available contact info. Already filtered to exclude companies you have.

**Card 3: Pre-Call Brief**

Badge: COMING SOON

Card headline: Pre-Call Brief — get ready in 30 seconds

Before: 15-20 minutes (searching emails, asking colleagues, digging through notes)
After: 30 seconds

Trigger: Ask what you know about a client. Get the brief.

Description: "What do we know about Rossi?" The agent returns: last contact, open offers, who's following them, recent notes. No email archaeology.

**Card 4: Follow-Up Radar**

Badge: COMING SOON

Card headline: Follow-Up Radar — no lead goes cold silently

Before: Deals die and nobody notices until the prospect has already bought from a competitor.
After: Morning alert: "Bianchi: 9 days no contact. Rossi: offer sent 14 days ago, no reply."

Trigger: Automatic every morning. Or on demand.

Description: The agent runs daily and sends you the list of leads you haven't contacted in more than 7 days. Nothing extra to do: it reads data you already have.

**Forbidden words check:** no occurrences of forbidden terms.

**Notes for the builder:**
- 4 cards in a 2x2 grid on desktop, vertical stack on mobile.
- LIVE cards: green border or badge (#10B981 or similar). COMING SOON cards: grey/secondary badge, text slightly more muted but still readable.
- Before/after numbers must be visually prominent: large body size, bold weight, amber color for the "after" value.
- LIVE / COMING SOON badge goes in the top right corner of each card.
- No decorative icons in the cards: the numbers are the visual content.

## Section 5: Differentiator (shared memory)

**Headline:**
What your rep logs today, your manager sees tonight.

**Subheadline / body direction:**
All agents work from the same memory. When a rep logs Bianchi Construction through Voice Lead, you can ask "what do we know about Bianchi?" that evening and get the full picture. No re-briefing, no "ask Marco what he knows," no copy-pasting between spreadsheets. The data goes in once and stays available to the whole team.

**CTA:** no direct CTA.

**Forbidden words check:** no occurrences of AREX, persistent context, or other forbidden terms.

**Notes for the builder:**
- This section differentiates Flowmeup from a single isolated agent.
- Tone: concrete, not technical. The "rep logs, manager sees" example is the core message.
- A simple minimal diagram showing the 4 agents connected to a shared center works if it stays clean. Otherwise text only.
- Slightly different background (muted navy or dark grey) to create visual breathing room.

## Section 6: Delivery

**Headline:**
Tell us your process. We configure. You're live within a week.

**Subheadline / body direction:**
Done for you. Your team installs nothing and attends no training. Flowmeup handles everything.

How it works: a 30-minute call where you tell us how your team operates. We configure the agents for your specific process. A 30-minute onboarding session for your team. From signed contract to first agent live: one week.

Changes you need afterward? Send a message. Implemented within 48 hours.

**CTA:** no direct CTA. Section leads naturally to pricing.

**Forbidden words check:** no occurrences of forbidden terms.

**Notes for the builder:**
- Consider a 3-4 step horizontal layout (numbered icon + short text) to make the process easy to scan.
- Suggested steps: 1. 30-min call, 2. Configuration, 3. Team onboarding, 4. Live.
- Emphasize "one week" — it's the differentiator vs. typical IT implementations.
- Tone: reassuring. The owner needs to know they don't touch anything themselves.

## Section 8: CTA / Contact

**Headline:**
Let's talk about your process.

**Subheadline / body direction:**
No pre-recorded demos. No self-serve trial. A 30-minute call where we understand how your team works and whether Flowmeup is the right fit.

**Form — 5 fields:**
1. Name
2. Company
3. Role
4. Sector
5. Message (free text: "Tell us briefly how your sales team operates")

**Form CTA button:**
"Send us your process"

(Rationale: "Send us your process" is more specific than "Book a call" — it communicates that the first step is describing your context, not just blocking a calendar slot. Keeps the discovery framing intact.)

**Calendar link:**
Text: "Or pick a time directly"
Link: Calendly or equivalent

**Forbidden words check:** no occurrences of forbidden terms.

**Notes for the builder:**
- Layout: form on the left (or on top on mobile), calendar link below or to the right.
- The Message field has a descriptive placeholder: "E.g. We have 4 field reps, we use Excel for leads, WhatsApp for team communication."
- Navy background for visual closure consistent with the hero.
- This section is the main conversion point. No distractions: no complex footer, no external links near the form.
- After submission: inline confirmation message (no redirect), text like "Got it. We'll write to you within 24 hours to confirm a time."
