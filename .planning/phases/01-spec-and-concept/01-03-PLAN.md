**The 8 sections:**

### Section 1: Hero
Starting point from spec.md: "Il tuo commerciale registra un lead in 90 secondi. Dal telefono. Mentre guida."
Write the headline (can use the spec draft or improve it), subheadline (1-2 sentences clarifying who this is for and what changes), and primary CTA button text.
The hero must answer in 5 seconds: Who is this for? What does it do? What do I do next?

### Section 2: Problem
Starting point from spec.md: "Ogni giorno i tuoi commerciali perdono ore ad aggiornare fogli e gestionali — quando va bene."
Body: Describe the problem viscerally. The rep finishes a meeting. He's in the car. The CRM is on the laptop at the office. He'll do it tonight. He doesn't. The lead goes cold. This is the daily reality. Write 3-4 short sentences, no jargon. First-person observation, not lecture.

### Section 3: Solution
Starting point from spec.md: "Agenti AI configurati sul tuo processo. Parlano con il tuo team. Salvano dove salvi già tu."
Body: Explain what the agents do in concrete terms. Voice note → record updated. No app to install. No training. Works with WhatsApp. Works with Excel. Works with what they already have. 3-4 sentences.

### Section 4: Agent Cards
Per SPEC-01: 4 cards. Voice Lead (LIVE), Lead Scout (LIVE), Pre-Call Brief (COMING SOON), Follow-Up Radar (COMING SOON).
For each card, write:
- Card headline (agent name + 1-line description)
- Before stat (e.g., "~30 min")
- After stat (e.g., "90 secondi")
- 1-sentence trigger description ("Manda una nota vocale. Il lead è salvato.")
Use actual numbers from spec.md section 4.

### Section 5: Differentiator (Shared Memory)
Starting point from spec.md: "Tutti gli agenti condividono la stessa memoria. Quello che registra il commerciale oggi, il titolare lo vede stasera."
IMPORTANT: Do NOT mention AREX. Do not use "contesto persistente."
Write: what the shared memory means in practice for the sales team. Rep logs Bianchi today. Manager asks "cosa sappiamo di Bianchi?" tonight. Gets the full picture. No re-briefing, no copy-paste, no "chiedilo a Mario."
2-3 short sentences, completely concrete.

### Section 6: Delivery
Starting point from spec.md: "Raccontaci il tuo processo. Noi configuriamo. Sei operativo entro una settimana."
Body: Done-for-you. Discovery call 30 minutes. Flowmeup sets up everything. Team walkthrough 30 minutes. Then it works. From signed contract to first agent: 1 week.
The copy should make the setup feel effortless, not technical.

### Section 7: Pricing
Headline: Something that frames pricing as transparent and simple (not "flexible" or "modular").
Body direction: Describe what to include in the pricing table. The table itself will be a structured component — this section just defines the copy framing.
Price per agent from spec.md: Voice Lead €59/mo, Lead Scout €99/mo, Pre-Call Brief €69/mo, Follow-Up Radar €49/mo. Setup fee €300 one-time.
Composition discounts: 2 agents -10%, 3 agents -15%, 4 agents -20%.
CTA: "Parliamo" or equivalent — not a "buy now" (this is a discovery-first sales process).
Note: Pricing is not yet validated against 5 discovery calls (per spec.md section 10). The copy should present prices clearly without pressure — conversational, not e-commerce.

### Section 8: CTA / Contact
Headline: "Parliamo del tuo processo" — or improved version.
Body: 1-2 sentences. No forms, no demos. Just a conversation. Discovery call 30 minutes.
Form fields (5): Nome, Azienda, Ruolo, Settore, Messaggio
CTA button: "Prenota una chiamata" or "Inviami il tuo processo" — choose the one that sounds more natural for the target.
Calendar link label: "Oppure scegli direttamente un orario" (links to Calendly or equivalent)
